Fig. 5(b): Simplified / Reduced MSTG (Human-Readable Paper View) Evaluator-Friendly

In the paper, the authors summarize complex internal states into clean, color-coded execution segments. Red lines mark cross-core system calls; colored bars show core tasks residing in their local Atomic Basic Blocks (ABBs).

SP (Synchronization Point): Meeting points where cores synchronize or call cross syscalls
Red Line: Cross-core system call (e.g. ActivateTask, GetSpinlock)
Yellow/Blue/Green Bars: Local computation ABBs executed independently on cores
Grey Intervals: Execution timing bounds used to prune unreachable states
Why this is easy to explain to examiners:
Instead of getting lost in 112 states, this diagram mirrors Figure 5(b) from page 12 of the paper. It shows the core lifecycle: 1. SP a starts the timeline. 2. Core 1 calls a cross syscall (red line) to awaken Core 0. 3. Core 0 and Core 1 execute their local tasks (blue and green bars) concurrently. 4. At SP c, the analyzer checks if they collide at the shared lock. Because their timing intervals [8–15ms] and [10–20ms] are safe, the lock is elided!
